# Green Tech Scholarship and Internship 2026

An opportunity to gain hands-on internship experience while receiving a scholarship at GREEN IN – a pioneer in green transition and Net Zero solutions 🌱

## Scholarship Value

04 scholarships | VND 18,000,000 / 6 months

## Eligibility and Internship Details

– Eligibility: Students majoring in Electrical Engineering & Renewable Energy; Applied Environmental Sciences

– Duration: 3–6 months (full-time) or 6 months (≥24 hours/week, flexible hybrid: online + offline)

– Focus areas: renewable energy, environmental issues, carbon credits, green transition
