# Procedures for Scholarships and Financial Aids Support

Article 8. Procedures

Procedures for scholarship and financial aids support for USTH students are followed 3 steps:

Step 1: widely publish and receive applications for scholarship and financial support.

From October 1st to 15th, the Student Services Department announces to all students about the application procedure for scholarships and supports.

Step 2: prepare proposed lists for scholarship and financial support; organize the SFA council and issue a final decision.

Within 15 days after the accepting deadline for students’ applications and proposals of Departments, the Student Services Department prepares lists and submits to the SFA council for finalization. Final decision is issued by the Rector of USTH.

Step 3: announce lists of students receiving scholarships and financial aids

In November, the Student Services Department and related departments are responsible for widely public the decision and list of students granted scholarships and financial aid support.

Scholarships and financial support will be bank-transferred 2 times/year after student’s completing the tuition fee payment.
